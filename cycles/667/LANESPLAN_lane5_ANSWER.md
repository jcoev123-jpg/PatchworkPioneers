TITLE: Prevent a reopened Peddler dye stop from accepting a previous visit's buy
MILESTONE: S6 — Stitched card-free split stops, Peddler Walk supplies
LANE: lane5

## Goal
Keep the existing useful Peddler dye purchase safe from an old click or outdated exact-state intent when an otherwise identical one-use visit is reopened (for example after Save/Reload). This is a two-file, scene-free change to existing working shop behavior, with no new gameplay control, price, shop stock, quote/forecast or art.

## Current behavior and exact rule
`scripts/run_stitched_peddler_dye.gd` is the already-live Stitched Peddler one-use purchase service. It admits exactly FOUR distinct ranked dolls (ranks 1–4), a pocket ledger keyed by exactly those four IDs (maximum TWO pocket items per doll), a nonnegative gold wallet and the run owner's STRICTLY POSITIVE integer quoted price. It offers `buy_dye` (put one literal `"dye"` in one non-LOST doll's free pocket, debit exactly the quote, consume the visit) or free `leave`. A purchase does not activate the dye's later Walk effect or consume RNG. The status states are invalid/open/used/left. Its current `buy_dye(hero_id, expected_node_id, expected_snapshot)` and `leave(expected_node_id, expected_snapshot)` guard on the exact `read_state()`, but two separately opened visits with identical node, wallet, roster, pockets and quote currently publish indistinguishable snapshots: no visit ID is present. Therefore an old same-node decision snapshot can authorize a new instance's purchase, with a visible unwanted wallet debit. Adjacent `RunStitchedPeddlerFreshStuffing` already demonstrates a process-local monotonic visit identity without clock or RNG.

## Implementation scope
Add a unique, process-local, no-RNG `visit_id` on successful admission to `RunStitchedPeddlerDye`; publish it in `read_state()` and an inert/empty ID in invalid state. Because this service already authenticates the entire snapshot, the genuine same-visit buy and Leave continue to work, while earlier/reopened snapshots fail closed even when all economic fields are identical. Leave the existing 11-key `quote()` structure and its established item-use description unchanged. Keep existing BUY/LEAVE receipts, quoted amount, eligibility, two-slot pocket cap, input/output detachment and once-only status unchanged. No modifications to another service or to run-session, save, corridor, UI, map graph, data, events, or any art.

## Acceptance criteria
AC1: Opening two valid independent `RunStitchedPeddlerDye` visits with identical node, ranked four-doll roster, pocket ledger, gold and positive quoted price produces distinct nonempty `read_state().visit_id` values, without calling the run RNG or changing the wallets, pockets, quotes or item choices. Existing invalid admission remains inert, with no usable visit ID. Prove in the existing headless service test.
AC2: After valid visit A has been left or bought, submitting its saved OPEN snapshot to a freshly opened but otherwise identical visit B fails both `buy_dye` and `leave` without spending gold, filling a pocket, or consuming B. B then accepts its own snapshot for exactly one genuine dye purchase or free Leave. Prove on same-node/same-ledger fixtures and on a forged `visit_id` in `tests/run_stitched_peddler_dye_test.gd`.
AC3: Authentic purchase still requires an eligible living/non-LOST doll, available pocket (0 or 1 existing items of maximum 2), sufficient wallet, matching node and exact current state. It appends just one `"dye"`, charges exactly the caller's positive integer price once, applies no Walk effect, and rejects stale/replayed/malformed/wrong-target/poor-wallet attempts. Existing `run_stitched_peddler_dye_test.gd` and routed `stitched_s7_routed_dye_live_test.gd` continue to pass without any weakened negative checks.

## Concurrency and owner exclusions
Use only `scripts/run_stitched_peddler_dye.gd` and extend the existing `tests/run_stitched_peddler_dye_test.gd`; those two paths are not in the current live task board's reservations. Do NOT touch the main cycle's `scripts/stitched/stitched_run_session.gd`, the S15 combat/save files, other lanes' enemy/event/field-guide files, any owner-owned corridor/visit/fight UI or art. Do not add new service quotes, numeric forecasts, Events or visible buttons, or retune body-part abilities. Preserve every existing price and effect: dye use later in the Walk may prevent the next fight's first strike and skip one curio trap; this purchase itself only stores the dye. Smallest necessary supporting corrections in affected existing regression tests are authorized if truly required, but preserve original negative/fail-closed checks; do not expand the feature or override concurrent ownership.

TITLE: Give each Stitched Peddler dye visit its own non-replayable identity
MILESTONE: S6 — Peddler one-use dye purchase, no corridor UI work
## Goal
Fix the existing Peddler dye transaction so an old choice snapshot from a previous same-node visit cannot buy dye or close a newly opened visit with identical economic state. Keep the authentic sale and its pocket item valuable and unchanged.

## Details (all rules/numbers/names needed, copied from the docs)
The Stitched run uses FOUR ranked ragdoll heroes, ranks 1–4; each hero owns a Walk pocket with capacity TWO. `RunStitchedPeddlerDye.open_stitched(node_id, heroes, pockets, gold, quoted_price)` is a scene-free opt-in Peddler service: `node_id` is a nonblank trimmed string; there must be four distinct hero IDs and ranks, each with a boolean `lost`; `pockets` is a Dictionary containing exactly those four IDs and Arrays of 0–2 legal IDs from `["thimble_tonic","fresh_stuffing","starch","dye","pins","spare_spool"]`. The caller supplies `gold` as a nonnegative INT and `quoted_price` as a positive INT. There is no hard-coded dye price; the test's existing valid example is 35 gold. Legal open choices are `buy_dye` if the wallet covers the caller quote and a not-LOST doll has a free pocket slot, plus `leave`. `buy_dye(hero_id, expected_node_id, expected_snapshot)` appends ONE literal `"dye"` to that doll only, charges the quote exactly once and ends the service. `leave(expected_node_id, expected_snapshot)` ends it without charging. Invalid, used, and left instances are inert. Purchase never changes HP, FRAY, candle, ranks, WHIRR, RNG or the Walk's later dye flags; those already belong to Walk consumption.

Concrete current defect: the service's `_matches_open` checks full snapshot equality and node ID but `read_state()` lacks an instance-bound ID. Two separate openings with exactly the same `node_id`, `heroes`, `pockets`, `gold` and `quoted_price` have equal OPEN snapshots. Thus an obsolete callback or saved choice from opening A can authorize `buy_dye` or `leave` on opening B. The related `scripts/run_stitched_peddler_fresh_stuffing.gd` uses `_next_serial` and `visit_id` to distinguish admissions without consuming random numbers. Reuse that small proven pattern: give successful `RunStitchedPeddlerDye` admissions distinct, nonempty per-instance `visit_id` values that become part of the authoritative `read_state()`. Keep invalid state's `visit_id` empty. Because `_matches_open` already compares the entire snapshot, it must then reject foreign snapshots with no extra caller parameter or new control. Identity need be unique only within the running process; do not use time, randomness or fragile hashes of economic state. Keep `quote()` at exactly its existing 11 keys, text and choices; this change must NOT create another service forecast. Do not alter existing receipt fields unless absolutely needed for unchanged behavior.

## Files likely touched
- `scripts/run_stitched_peddler_dye.gd`: add successful-admission visit identity, expose it in valid and invalid read_state, retain full-state guard and all sale/Leave semantics.
- `tests/run_stitched_peddler_dye_test.gd`: extend its existing SceneTree service test with dual-visit identical-economic-state stale-intent witnesses, ID detachment and RNG invariant; do not create a new test file.
Read-only pattern and regression references: `scripts/run_stitched_peddler_fresh_stuffing.gd`, `tests/run_stitched_peddler_fresh_stuffing_test.gd`, `tests/stitched_s7_routed_dye_live_test.gd`.
